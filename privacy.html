Privacy policy.

How information may be collected and used when you interact with GetInfo Reach.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide through enquiry, booking or account forms, such as your name, email address, phone number and project details.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, manage appointments, improve our website and communicate important updates.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers who help operate our website or deliver services, where appropriate safeguards apply.

4. Data security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@getinforeach.com.

7. Website and technical information

When a person visits a website, technical information such as browser type, device information, pages requested and basic server logs may be processed by the hosting provider. Whether this information is collected depends on the hosting and security tools used.

8. Retention

Personal information should be kept only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or for a period required by applicable law. A retention schedule should be defined for enquiries, client records and accounting information.

9. Children

The website and services are intended for businesses and general audiences. Do not knowingly submit personal information about a child unless there is a valid reason and appropriate permission.

10. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features, service providers or business practices change. The revised version should show its effective date.

11. Questions or requests

For a privacy request, explain what information or interaction your request relates to so it can be reviewed. Replace the sample email address with a monitored business contact before publishing.

This is a starter policy template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed and adapted to your actual business practices and applicable laws before publishing.
