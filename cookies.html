Information about cookies and similar technologies used on this website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored by your browser that can help a website remember preferences and understand how visitors use its pages.

How cookies may be used

This website may use essential cookies for basic functionality and, if enabled, analytics cookies to understand website traffic.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality.

Your preferences

Analytics or marketing cookies should only be activated in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

5. Types of cookies

Depending on the tools installed, cookies may be session-based and expire when a browser closes, or persistent and remain for a defined period. They may also be first-party cookies set by the website or third-party cookies set by an external service.

6. Analytics and advertising tools

If analytics, embedded media, chat tools or advertising pixels are added later, they may collect or receive information about website activity. Review each provider's documentation and configure consent controls where required before enabling them.

7. Browser controls

Most browsers allow visitors to view, block or delete cookies. Settings differ by browser and device. Blocking essential cookies may affect some website features.

8. Policy changes

This page should be updated whenever the website's cookie use changes. Keep a record of the tools in use and the purpose of each category.
